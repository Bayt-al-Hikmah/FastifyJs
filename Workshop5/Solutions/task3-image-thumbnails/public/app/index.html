Upload a JPEG or PNG to a task. The server validates the real file signature, stores the original behind authentication, and generates a metadata-free thumbnail that is never larger than 300 pixels wide.

300 px maximum thumbnail width
24 MP decoder pixel ceiling
10 MB per-file upload limit

Files are written to private directories under var/uploads. Nothing in the upload tree is served statically.

Access tokens last 15 minutes and are held in this browser.
